## Is Autoship better than using a Chewy promo code?

That depends on the product and the order size.

A one-time coupon is usually better when:

- You are making a single purchase.
- The code applies to your entire eligible subtotal.
- You do not want recurring deliveries.
- The item is not eligible for Autoship.
- You are buying a higher-priced product with a capped Autoship discount.

Autoship is usually better when:

- You buy the same item repeatedly.
- The item receives the 5% recurring discount.
- The first-order discount reaches the $20 maximum.
- You want a predictable refill schedule.
- Your pet uses prescription medication or a regular diet product.

Consider a $60 eligible Autoship order. A 35% first-order discount would theoretically be $21, but the $20 maximum means the first-order savings stop at $20. The important detail is to check whether the product is actually marked as Autoship eligible. A product can have a regular sale price without receiving the same discount through recurring delivery.

## What to do when a promo code does not work

Start with the cart, not another coupon website.

First, remove excluded products temporarily and see whether the offer applies to the remaining eligible items. Then check the subtotal before tax and shipping. If the promotion requires a $49 minimum, a cart that reaches $49 before a discount may fall below the threshold after the discount is applied, depending on the offer terms.

Next, review the account status. Many new-customer promotions are limited to customers with no prior Chewy order history. A household may also be limited to one promotion, and a phone number, address, payment method, or previous account can affect eligibility.

For pharmacy promotions, verify the prescription category and product exclusions. The official pharmacy terms specifically list excluded products and require the first eligible pharmacy purchase to use Autoship.

If the code still fails:

- Check capitalization and spacing.
- Remove another promo code.
- Confirm that the promotion has not expired.
- Look for an automatic offer in the cart.
- Try the promotion on an eligible item rather than a mixed cart.
- Contact Chewy customer service before placing the order if the offer was shown in your account.

A failed code does not necessarily mean the product is not discounted. Chewy may already have applied an item-level sale, Autoship price, or account promotion.
